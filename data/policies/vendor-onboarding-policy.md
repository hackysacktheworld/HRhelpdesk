# Vendor Onboarding and Risk Review Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the process for onboarding new vendors and reviewing vendor risk before company funds, systems, or data are shared. The goal is to prevent avoidable financial, security, privacy, and operational risk.

## 2. When Vendor Review Is Required
Vendor review is required before signing a contract, issuing a purchase order, sharing confidential information, granting system access, processing customer data, or paying a new vendor.

Renewals require review when the contract value increases by more than 20 percent, the vendor will receive new data types, the service scope changes materially, or the previous review is more than 18 months old.

## 3. Required Intake Information
The requester must provide the vendor name, business purpose, estimated annual spend, data types involved, systems requiring access, contract term, department owner, and whether the vendor will interact with customers.

## 4. Spend Thresholds
Vendors below 2,500 USD annually may follow the lightweight review process unless they receive confidential, personal, payment, or production system data.

Vendors from 2,500 USD to 25,000 USD annually require Procurement review and manager approval. Vendors above 25,000 USD annually require Procurement, Legal, Finance, and department leader approval.

## 5. Security and Privacy Review
Security review is required when a vendor will access company systems, customer data, employee personal data, production environments, source code, or confidential business information.

Privacy review is required when a vendor processes personal data, tracks individuals, supports marketing communications, or stores customer interaction history.

## 6. Contract Requirements
Contracts must include confidentiality obligations, data protection terms when applicable, service description, pricing, termination rights, renewal terms, and support obligations. Legal must approve changes to liability, indemnity, privacy, or data processing terms.

## 7. Prohibited Vendor Activity
Vendors may not receive authentication secrets, unrestricted production access, customer data exports, or admin credentials without documented approval from Security and the department owner.

## 8. Offboarding
When a vendor relationship ends, the vendor owner must confirm access removal, data return or deletion, final invoice status, and contract closure within 10 business days.
