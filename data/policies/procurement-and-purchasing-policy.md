# Procurement and Purchasing Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how employees request purchases, obtain approvals, and manage company spending. It is intended to support responsible purchasing, budget visibility, and vendor accountability.

## 2. Purchase Requests
Employees must submit a purchase request before committing company funds unless the purchase is covered by an approved purchasing card exception. A purchase request should include business purpose, vendor, estimated cost, budget owner, requested start date, and whether a contract is involved.

## 3. Approval Thresholds
Purchases below 500 USD require manager approval. Purchases from 500 USD to 5,000 USD require manager and budget owner approval. Purchases above 5,000 USD require manager, budget owner, and Finance approval.

Purchases above 25,000 USD require Procurement review and department leader approval. Multi-year commitments require Finance review regardless of annual cost.

## 4. Competitive Quotes
Purchases above 10,000 USD should include at least two competitive quotes unless Procurement approves a sole-source justification. A sole-source justification must explain why the vendor is uniquely qualified or why switching costs make alternatives impractical.

## 5. Software Purchases
Software purchases require IT review when the software needs system access, stores company data, processes customer data, integrates with company systems, or requires installation on company-managed devices.

Software purchases also require Security and Privacy review when the vendor will process Confidential, Restricted, customer, or employee personal data.

## 6. Contracts
Employees may not sign vendor contracts unless they have delegated signing authority. Contracts must be reviewed by Legal before signature unless an approved standard agreement is used without changes.

## 7. Purchasing Cards
Purchasing cards may be used only for approved business expenses and must not be used to split purchases to avoid approval thresholds. Cardholders must submit receipts and business purpose details within 10 business days.

## 8. Emergency Purchases
Emergency purchases may proceed without normal pre-approval only when delay would create immediate operational, customer, security, or safety harm. The requester must document the reason and complete retroactive approval within two business days.
