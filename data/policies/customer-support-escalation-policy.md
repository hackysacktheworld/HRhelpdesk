# Customer Support Escalation Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines when customer support issues should be escalated, who should receive the escalation, and what information must be included. The goal is to resolve urgent issues quickly and maintain a consistent customer experience.

## 2. Severity Levels
Severity 1 issues include total service outage, confirmed data loss, active security incident, major customer-impacting billing failure, or a production issue affecting more than 25 percent of active customers.

Severity 2 issues include degraded service, repeated failures in a core workflow, incorrect account access, delayed customer onboarding for a strategic account, or billing errors affecting a single customer above 5,000 USD.

Severity 3 issues include non-urgent product bugs, documentation questions, minor billing questions, and feature requests.

## 3. Escalation Timing
Severity 1 issues must be escalated immediately through the incident channel and acknowledged by an incident lead within 15 minutes. Severity 2 issues must be escalated within one business hour. Severity 3 issues should follow the standard ticket queue.

## 4. Required Escalation Details
Each escalation must include customer name, account ID, summary of the issue, impact, severity level, steps already taken, screenshots or logs when available, requested outcome, and the support agent's recommended next action.

## 5. Security and Privacy Concerns
Any support issue involving suspected unauthorized access, exposed personal data, phishing, compromised credentials, or customer data sent to the wrong recipient must be escalated to Security immediately.

Support agents must not request passwords, full payment card numbers, or authentication secrets from customers.

## 6. Executive Visibility
Support leads must notify the executive sponsor when a Severity 1 issue affects a strategic account, receives media attention, or creates material contractual risk.

## 7. Customer Communication
Customer communication should be clear, factual, and calm. Agents should not speculate about root cause, make compensation promises, or commit to a fix date unless approved by the incident lead or account owner.

## 8. Closure Requirements
Escalated issues may be closed only after the customer impact is resolved or mitigated, internal owners agree on the next step, and the ticket includes a summary of actions taken.
