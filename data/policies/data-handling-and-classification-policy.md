# Data Handling and Classification Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how company data should be classified, stored, shared, and protected. The goal is to reduce the risk of unauthorized disclosure, loss, or misuse.

## 2. Data Classification Levels
Public data is approved for public release, such as published marketing materials and public documentation.

Internal data is intended for company use but would create limited harm if disclosed, such as internal meeting notes or project plans.

Confidential data would create business, legal, customer, or employee harm if disclosed. Examples include customer records, employee records, product roadmaps, pricing strategy, source code, and non-public financial information.

Restricted data requires the highest protection. Examples include payment card data, authentication secrets, government identifiers, health information, private keys, and incident investigation records.

## 3. Storage Requirements
Confidential and Restricted data must be stored only in approved company systems. Employees may not store Confidential or Restricted data in personal cloud drives, personal email, removable media, or unapproved AI tools.

Restricted data must be encrypted at rest and in transit. Access must be limited to users with a documented business need.

## 4. Sharing Requirements
Confidential data may be shared internally only with employees who need it for their role. External sharing requires a business purpose, approved recipient, and appropriate agreement such as a confidentiality agreement or data processing agreement.

Restricted data may not be shared externally unless Legal, Security, and the data owner approve the transfer in writing.

## 5. Data Minimization
Employees should collect, process, and retain only the minimum data necessary for the business purpose. When possible, use aggregated, anonymized, redacted, or synthetic data instead of personal or customer-identifiable data.

## 6. Retention and Deletion
Data should be retained according to the applicable retention schedule. Employees must not delete records subject to a legal hold, audit request, or active investigation.

## 7. AI Tool Usage
Employees must follow the Responsible AI Usage Policy before entering company data into any AI system. Confidential or Restricted data may be used with AI only when the system is approved for that data type and the use case has completed required review.

## 8. Reporting Data Exposure
Suspected data exposure must be reported to Security immediately. Employees should preserve relevant evidence and avoid notifying external parties until Security and Legal provide instructions.
