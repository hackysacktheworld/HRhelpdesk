# Remote Work and Device Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains expectations for remote work, company devices, workspace security, and employee availability. It supports flexible work while protecting company data and customer trust.

## 2. Remote Work Eligibility
Remote work eligibility depends on role requirements, manager approval, performance expectations, legal location restrictions, and business needs. Some roles may require scheduled office presence for customer, security, hardware, or team collaboration reasons.

## 3. Work Location
Employees must keep their work location current in the HR system. Working from a new state or country for more than 10 business days requires People Operations approval before travel begins.

## 4. Availability
Remote employees are expected to maintain normal working hours, attend required meetings, respond to urgent messages in a timely manner, and coordinate schedule changes with their manager.

## 5. Device Requirements
Employees must use company-managed devices for company work unless IT approves an exception. Company devices must use full-disk encryption, endpoint protection, password or biometric lock, automatic screen lock, and current security updates.

Personal devices may not be used to store Confidential or Restricted data. Employees may not share company devices with family members, friends, or unauthorized users.

## 6. Network and Workspace Security
Employees should use secure networks and avoid public Wi-Fi for sensitive work unless connected through approved security controls. Employees should prevent shoulder surfing and should lock screens when stepping away.

Printed company documents must be secured and shredded when no longer needed.

## 7. Lost or Stolen Devices
Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to IT and Security immediately. The employee should provide device type, last known location, time discovered, and whether the device may contain Confidential or Restricted data.

## 8. Equipment and Reimbursement
Remote work equipment reimbursement follows the Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy. Employees should obtain required approvals before purchasing home office equipment above the relevant threshold.
