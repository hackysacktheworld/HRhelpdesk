# Health Benefits Enrollment Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains how eligible employees enroll in company health benefits, when coverage begins, which qualifying life events may allow benefit changes, and where employees can get help. The company provides health benefits to support employee wellbeing and access to care.

## 2. Eligibility
Regular full-time employees are eligible for company-sponsored health benefits. Regular part-time employees may be eligible depending on scheduled hours, location, and plan rules. Temporary employees, interns, and contractors are not eligible unless required by law or a written agreement.

Eligibility rules may vary by country, state, employment classification, and benefits plan. Employees should contact Benefits Administration if they are unsure whether they qualify.

## 3. New Hire Enrollment Window
New hires must enroll in health benefits within 30 calendar days of their benefits eligibility date. The eligibility date is usually the employee's start date unless the offer letter or local policy states otherwise.

Employees who miss the new hire enrollment window may need to wait until open enrollment unless they experience a qualifying life event.

## 4. How To Enroll
Employees should enroll through the benefits portal. The employee should review available medical, dental, and vision plans, confirm covered dependents, select coverage levels, designate beneficiaries when applicable, and submit elections before the enrollment deadline.

After submitting elections, employees should save or download the confirmation page. Benefits Administration may request additional documentation for dependent coverage.

## 5. Coverage Start Date
For eligible new hires who enroll on time, coverage usually begins on the benefits eligibility date. Some plans may have a different start date based on plan rules, location, or employment classification.

Employees should check the benefits portal confirmation and plan documents for the official coverage start date.

## 6. Qualifying Life Events
Employees may change benefit elections outside open enrollment only after a qualifying life event. Common qualifying life events include marriage, divorce, birth or adoption of a child, loss of other coverage, gain of other coverage, change in dependent eligibility, and certain employment status changes.

Employees must submit qualifying life event changes within 30 calendar days of the event unless local law or plan rules allow a different deadline.

## 7. Dependent Documentation
The company may require documentation to verify dependent eligibility. Examples include marriage certificates, birth certificates, adoption records, or proof of loss of other coverage.

Employees should upload requested documents through the benefits portal by the stated deadline. Failure to provide documentation may delay or cancel dependent coverage.

## 8. Open Enrollment
Open enrollment is the annual period when eligible employees may change benefit elections for the next plan year. People Operations will announce open enrollment dates, deadlines, plan changes, and employee information sessions.

Employees who do not make changes during open enrollment may keep existing elections when plan rules allow, but some elections may require active confirmation each year.

## 9. Benefit Questions and Support
Employees with questions about eligibility, enrollment deadlines, plan comparisons, dependent documentation, or coverage start dates should contact Benefits Administration or use the benefits portal help center.

Managers should not provide detailed benefits advice. Managers should direct employees to Benefits Administration for plan-specific or personal coverage questions.

## 10. Privacy
Benefits information may include personal or health-related data and must be handled according to the Data Handling and Classification Policy. Employees should not share benefits documents through unapproved systems.
