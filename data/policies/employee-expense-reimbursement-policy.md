# Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains which business expenses are eligible for reimbursement, what documentation is required, and how employees should submit reimbursement requests. The goal is to support reasonable business spending while maintaining consistent financial controls.

## 2. Eligible Expenses
Employees may request reimbursement for expenses that are ordinary, necessary, pre-approved when required, and directly related to company business. Eligible categories include business travel, client meals, approved training, professional certifications, home office equipment, and software subscriptions used for company work.

## 3. Home Office Equipment
Employees may request reimbursement for home office equipment when the equipment is required to perform their role effectively. Eligible items may include monitors, keyboards, mice, webcams, headsets, ergonomic chairs, and laptop stands.

Home office equipment purchases over 150 USD require manager approval before purchase. Items over 500 USD require approval from both the manager and Finance Operations. The company may treat reimbursed equipment as company property and may request return of the item when employment ends.

## 4. Professional Certifications and Training
Role-related certifications, courses, and conference registrations may be reimbursed when approved by the employee's manager before purchase. Reimbursement is more likely when the training supports current responsibilities, a documented development plan, or a business priority.

Exam retakes are reimbursable only when the employee has prior manager approval. Optional study materials are limited to 200 USD per certification unless Finance grants an exception.

## 5. Travel and Meals
Business travel must be approved before booking. Employees should select reasonable economy travel options and use preferred vendors when available. Meals during approved business travel are reimbursable up to 75 USD per day, excluding alcohol.

Client meals are reimbursable when there is a clear business purpose and the attendees are documented. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless approved in advance by an executive.

## 6. Required Documentation
Each reimbursement request must include an itemized receipt, date of purchase, vendor name, amount, business purpose, and approval evidence when pre-approval is required. Credit card statements alone are not sufficient unless Finance approves an exception.

## 7. Submission Deadline
Employees should submit reimbursement requests within 30 calendar days of purchase. Requests submitted after 60 calendar days may be denied unless the employee provides a documented reason and receives Finance approval.

## 8. Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The company does not reimburse personal entertainment, commuting costs, parking tickets, personal clothing, fines, unauthorized software, gift cards, or expenses lacking a valid business purpose.

## 9. Exceptions
Exceptions must be documented in writing and approved by Finance Operations. Managers may recommend exceptions but may not approve exceptions on behalf of Finance.
